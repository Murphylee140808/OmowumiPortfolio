About Me

The Designer Behind the Work

I'm Akindehinde Omowumi — a no-code website designer and digital solutions specialist based in Nigeria, helping businesses, entrepreneurs, coaches, and brands build websites that don't just look stunning, but actively generate leads, bookings, and sales.

With deep expertise in Squarespace, Wix, WordPress, and AI-powered builders like Lovable, Base44, and Replit, I design websites that are clean, fast, mobile-perfect, and conversion-focused. My work spans real estate, wellness, e-commerce, coaching, healthcare, law, beauty, and more.

Clients come to me when their current site feels fine but isn't performing. I redesign the structure, fix the mobile experience, improve SEO, and turn passive websites into active business tools.

Beyond my work in digital solutions, I am also a Nigerian Army Veteran, having honorably served in the Nigerian Army. This experience shaped my discipline, resilience, sense of responsibility, and commitment to excellence — qualities I bring into every project I take on.
